About

Scientist, research leader and partnership builder, now Vice-Chancellor of the university where she began. Open a card below to read more.

Prof Thandi Mgwebi is Vice-Chancellor and Principal of iYunivesithi Walter Sisulu, and former Group Executive for Business Advancement and Global Partnerships at the National Research Foundation. She brings more than two decades of leadership in research management and higher education.

Her academic training spans universities in South Africa, Australia and the United Kingdom, complemented by postdoctoral research and executive education. Her leadership has been shaped by senior roles at South African universities, as Deputy Vice-Chancellor for Research at Nelson Mandela University and Tshwane University of Technology, and as Director of Research at the University of the Western Cape.

She is an influential voice in science and development, serving on strategic boards and councils, including the Leadership Council of the South African chapter of the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN).

    Leadership

    The themes that run through her work across universities and national science bodies.

    Research and innovation

    Two terms as a Deputy Vice-Chancellor responsible for research and innovation, and years running national programmes that fund South Africa's research chairs and centres of excellence.

    Global partnerships

    Partnerships with international funders and networks such as Fulbright, the British Council, the Newton Fund, RUFORUM and SASUF, bringing support to the national science agenda.

    People and capacity

    A long record in human capital development: building the next generation of researchers, and championing gender inclusion across the science system.

    Universities serving their regions

    Linking universities, government and industry, with a focus on what a comprehensive university can do for the communities of the Eastern Cape.
